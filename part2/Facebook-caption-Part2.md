Plan G or Plan N? On paper, they look almost identical. But two differences decide which one fits you better.

1. Copays. Plan N usually has a lower monthly premium, but you may pay up to $20 at some office visits and up to $50 at the ER. Plan G doesn't have those copays.

2. Excess charges. Some doctors can bill up to 15% above Medicare's rate. Plan G covers that. Plan N does not.

So which one is for you?
If you rarely see the doctor and want a lower premium, Plan N may fit.
If you want predictable costs with no surprise bills, Plan G may fit.
